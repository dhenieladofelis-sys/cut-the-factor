📚 MINI LESSON

What is a Perfect Square Trinomial?

A perfect square trinomial can be factored into the square of a binomial.

a² + 2ab + b² = (a + b)²
a² − 2ab + b² = (a − b)²

Example: x² + 6x + 9

Step 1 – Label each term:
x² = (a), 6x = (b), 9 = (c)
Step 2 – Check the first and last terms:
x² is the square of x. 9 is the square of 3. Both are perfect squares.
Step 3 – Get the square roots:
√x² = x and √9 = 3
Step 4 – Double the product:
2(x)(3) = 6x. This matches the middle term.
Step 5 – Factor:
x² + 6x + 9 = (x + 3)²
